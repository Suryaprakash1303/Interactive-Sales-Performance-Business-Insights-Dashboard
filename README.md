# 📊 Interactive Sales Performance & Business Insights Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing sales performance and generating meaningful business insights using Microsoft Power BI. The interactive dashboard helps understand sales trends, profitability, product category performance, regional sales, and sales channel effectiveness.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify sales trends and patterns.
- Compare performance across product categories.
- Evaluate regional sales distribution.
- Analyze sales channel performance.
- Provide interactive reports for better business decision-making.

## 📂 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Performance Analysis
- Daily Sales Trend
- Monthly Sales Comparison
- Sales by Day of Week
- KPI Cards for key business metrics

### 2. Category Performance Analysis
- Sales by Category
- Category Sales Distribution
- Profit by Category

### 3. Regional Sales Performance
- Sales by Region
- Profit by Region
- Regional Sales Contribution

### 4. Sales Channel Performance
- Sales by Channel
- Profit by Channel
- Channel Sales Distribution

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence

## ⚙️ Key Features
- Interactive slicers and filters
- Dynamic KPI cards
- Bookmark-based navigation
- Multiple analytical dashboard pages
- Reset Filters functionality
- Consistent dark-themed dashboard design

## 📈 Business Insights
The dashboard is designed to help users:
- Monitor sales and profitability.
- Identify high-performing categories and regions.
- Compare different sales channels.
- Explore sales trends over time.
- Support data-driven business decisions.
